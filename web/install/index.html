Safar

Your train, your berth, your platform. سفر، آسان

Book Pakistan Railways in a few taps, watch your train move between stops, and carry a ticket that still scans when there is no signal anywhere near the Bolan.

Install the app

Tickets that work offline

Your PNR, coach and berth stay on the phone, and the barcode is generated on the device and rotates every 45 seconds.

Watch your train move

A live map with the train pointing the way it is going, tracking stop by stop from Karachi to Peshawar.

Told before your stop

Alerts when the journey starts, a few minutes before you arrive, and again when you are there.

Delays and platforms

Delay minutes from the control office and the platform number as soon as the station sets it, including late changes.

Pick the right class

Economy to AC Sleeper, with berths left, what is on board, and how full the coaches already are.

The last mile

Metrobus, BRT, People's Bus, rickshaw and Careem from the station you arrive at, ranked by what you can actually catch.

Printing a station poster?
Encode this exact URL. It detects the device and sends each person to the right place, so one code covers every platform and never needs reprinting when a store listing moves.
